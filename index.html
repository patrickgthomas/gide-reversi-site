Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Place a disc so that it traps a line of the other color between your new disc and one of yours. The trapped discs flip to your color. When the board fills, or neither side can move, the most discs wins.

What happens if I can't move?

Your turn is skipped and the message line tells you so. If neither side can move, the game ends.

Is this Othello?

It's the same classic game, also known as Reversi.

Can I play against a person?

Yes. Choose Two players in Settings and pass the phone after each turn.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, and nothing leaves your device.

How do I get support?
